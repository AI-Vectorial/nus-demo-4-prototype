## What we are building

A staff member types a policy question and gets one answer, with the policy sections it was
written from beside it. If the corpus cannot support an answer, the product says so, points to
Group Finance where that applies, and does not call the model. The model only writes the answer
from passages it was handed; with generation switched off, the product still works as a plain
policy search. Follow-ups, the corpus browser and history are built after the core, and any of
them that do not survive review are cut from the interface too.

## Why it matters

Staff lose about 18 minutes per routine policy question and about 61% of them interrupt a
colleague. One answer in six in the audit sample cited superseded policy. If this does not
exist, staff keep guessing which policy is current, and the wrong answer is only found by audit.
