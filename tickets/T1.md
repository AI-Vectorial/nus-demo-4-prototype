## What we are building

A staff member types one policy question and gets one answer, composed only from
Aldermere's policy corpus, with the sections it came from shown beside it. If the corpus
cannot support an answer, the assistant says so, says who to ask, and does not call the
model. If the model is off, the passages still come back.

## Why it matters

Today roughly one policy answer in six cites a superseded version, and nobody finds out
until an audit does. Without this, staff keep acting on the fastest answer rather than
the current one — and the one thing worse than that is a tool that answers confidently
from nothing.

## Acceptance criteria

*Every line is pass or fail. No "properly", "correctly", "well". Someone who
has not read the PRD should be able to check each one.*

- [ ] A refusal arrives in under 1.5 seconds, because a refusal makes no model call and should be visibly faster   *(REQ-R3, NFR-2)*
- [ ] Citations are always visible without clicking anything   *(REQ-U2)*
- [ ] A claim of exactly £75 requires approval — thresholds are inclusive. Asked "Does a claim of exactly £75 need approval?", the assistant answers yes   *(REQ-R5, REQ-G1)*
- [ ] A superseded citation is visibly marked as superseded   *(REQ-U4, REQ-I7)*
- [ ] With generation off, the passages still come back, clearly flagged   *(REQ-G6, REQ-U6, NFR-3)*
- [ ] The top bar carries Reset only in this slice — no History or Corpus button   *(REQ-U9, deviation below)*
- [ ] `/healthz` answers when Postgres or Qdrant is down   *(REQ-A5, NFR-5)*
