## What we are building

An assistant for Aldermere Bank staff who want to check a policy without searching
through the policy documents themselves. They ask in plain language and get a clear
answer drawn from the relevant policy sections, with citations showing where it came
from. When the policies do not support an answer, it says so instead of making
something up.

## Why it matters

Without it, staff keep interrupting colleagues or acting on a stale email. A
confident answer the policies do not support is worse than no answer, so the refusal
is as much the product as the answer is.

## Acceptance criteria

*Written by the team. Every line is pass or fail.*

### Ingestion (F1)

- [ ] **C1.** Running `python -m app.ingest` on an empty collection indexes every document in `backend/corpus/`, including the superseded travel policy v3.0.   *(REQ-I1, I7)*
- [ ] **C2.** Running it a second time leaves the passage count and passage ids unchanged.   *(REQ-I4)*
- [ ] **C3.** Every indexed passage carries its document id, title, section, version and status.   *(REQ-I3)*
- [ ] **C29.** After `python -m app.ingest --force`, the collection has been recreated and every passage re-embedded, with the same passage count and ids as before. During a full ingest, the number of embedding requests is less than the number of passages, and no request carries more than `EMBEDDING_BATCH_SIZE` passages.   *(REQ-I5, I6)*

### Answers and refusals (F2 to F4)

- [ ] **C4.** For each of the 10 answerable evaluation questions (Q1 to Q10), the expected section appears in the top three retrieved results and the app returns a grounded answer, not a refusal. For Q10, the criterion is only that both conflicting sections are retrieved; how the conflict is presented is not fixed here and is decided at stage 02.   *(REQ-R5, G1)*
- [ ] **C5.** For each of the 3 out-of-scope evaluation questions (Q11 to Q13), the app returns a refusal.   *(REQ-R6)*
- [ ] **C6.** A refusal says the available policies do not support the question and says where to go instead. It contains no invented answer and is clearly different from an error.   *(REQ-R3, U5)*
- [ ] **C7.** When retrieval is insufficient, no model call is made. A test using the fake model asserts `was_called` is false.   *(REQ-R2, R3)*
- [ ] **C8.** With `GENERATION_ENABLED=false`, or with the failing fake model, the app returns the retrieved passages, no composed answer, and an explicit degraded outcome.   *(REQ-G6)*
- [ ] **C9.** The UI shows a degraded response as visibly degraded. It does not look like a normal answer or an error.   *(REQ-U6)*
- [ ] **C30.** For Q10, the answer cites both Outsourcing v1.8 §6.4 and Records Retention v4.0 §4.1, says the two requirements conflict, names the escalation to Group Legal and Group Operational Risk, and does not present either requirement as the one that applies.   *(REQ-G1, D6)*

### Citations and the interface (F5)

- [ ] **C10.** Every answer shows its citations without any extra click. Each citation shows document, section, version and status.   *(REQ-G3, U2, U3)*
- [ ] **C11.** A superseded citation is visibly marked as superseded.   *(REQ-U4)*
- [ ] **C12.** Clicking a citation opens the policy section it names.   *(REQ-U15)*
- [ ] **C13.** I can ask a question in the browser and see the question, a pending state, and then the answer with citations.   *(REQ-U1, U2, U7)*

### Follow-ups (F6, cuttable)

- [ ] **C14.** After "What is the approval limit for travel expenses?", asking "What about expenses above that limit?" gives an answer that understands the earlier context, and retrieval runs again on the follow-up. What text retrieval runs on is not fixed here and is decided at stage 02.   *(REQ-C3, C5)*
- [ ] **C15.** Reset starts a new conversation with no prior context. Saved history is not deleted.   *(REQ-C6)*
- [ ] **C31.** Asking Q11 ("Can I expense my partner's flight if they travel with me?") as a follow-up straight after Q1 returns a refusal, not an answer.   *(REQ-R6, REQ-C7, D9)*

### Corpus browser (F7, cuttable)

- [ ] **C16.** `/corpus` lists all five documents with title, owner, version, effective date and status, with the superseded one visibly marked.   *(REQ-B1, B2)*
- [ ] **C17.** `/corpus/[id]` shows a document's sections and their text.   *(REQ-B3)*
- [ ] **C18.** The section opened from a citation is the same section that was cited.   *(REQ-U15)*
- [ ] **C26.** Policy tables render as tables, not as raw pipes and dashes.   *(REQ-U12)*

### History (F8, cuttable)

- [ ] **C19.** `/history` lists past conversations, newest first.   *(REQ-C8, U13)*
- [ ] **C20.** Opening a past conversation shows its questions and answers. I can then ask a follow-up that still has its context.   *(REQ-C9, U13)*

### Quality and boundaries

- [ ] **C21.** Median answer latency is under 5 seconds and median refusal latency is under 1.5 seconds, measured on the running app.   *(NFR-1, NFR-2)*
- [ ] **C22.** The text of a user's question is never written to normal application logs. Troubleshooting information that does not include question content is still logged. The question is stored in Postgres, because conversation history requires storing turns; the rule is about application logs only.   *(NFR-6)*
- [ ] **C23.** The full test suite passes, with no test skipped, weakened or deleted.
- [ ] **C25.** The top bar only offers actions whose routes exist: Reset always, Corpus once F7 exists, History once F8 exists. No button leads nowhere.   *(REQ-U9)*
- [ ] **C27.** The interface uses the light theme only and works at phone width.   *(REQ-U10, U11)*
- [ ] **C28.** `/healthz` still responds and reports each dependency when Postgres or Qdrant is unavailable.   *(REQ-A5, NFR-5)*

## Open questions

- **How a conflict between two current policies is presented (D6).** Decided at stage 02: surface both, do not resolve. See C30 and `docs/design/D6-conflicts.md`.
- **What text a follow-up retrieves on (D9).** Decided at stage 02: the user questions within the last `CONTEXT_MESSAGES` messages. See C31 and `docs/design/D9-follow-up-retrieval.md`.
- **PRD wording: REQ-C4 says "turns", §6 says "rows".** We follow §6 (messages). The PRD is protected; the wording issue is for its owner. See `docs/design/D8-context-window.md`.
- **One threshold may not separate all 13 evaluation questions.** All 13 is the bar. If no threshold passes all 13, we stop and say so rather than adjusting anything.
- **Latency (C21) needs the running app**, so it is checked in stage 06 as well as during the build.
