## What we are building

A staff member asks one policy question and gets one answer that cites the policy sections it came from.
If the corpus cannot support an answer, the product refuses and does not call the model.

## Who it is for, and the moment they need it

Operations and front-office staff, mid-task, with a policy question in the way of the job.
A relationship manager with a client on the phone needs an answer in under a minute, with its source visible.

## Why it matters

Today there is no single place to find current policy. Staff interrupt colleagues (61% of the time) or act on superseded email, and roughly one answer in six cites a superseded version.

## What we are NOT building

- A general-purpose chatbot. It answers from the corpus or it refuses.
- Any decision about whether an exception is permitted.
- Editing or authoring policy.

## Acceptance criteria

*Every line is pass or fail. No "properly", "correctly", "well". Someone who
has not read the PRD should be able to check each one.*

- [ ] Running `python -m app.ingest` on an empty collection indexes every passage from all five documents in `backend/corpus/`. *(REQ-I1, REQ-I2)*
- [ ] Each passage carries document id, title, section, version and status. *(REQ-I3)*
- [ ] The superseded travel policy v3.0 is indexed with `status: superseded`, not excluded. *(REQ-I7)*
- [ ] Running ingestion a second time leaves the passage count and passage ids unchanged. *(REQ-I4)*
- [ ] `--force` recreates the collection and re-embeds everything. *(REQ-I5)*
- [ ] Embedding calls are sent in batches of `EMBEDDING_BATCH_SIZE`. *(REQ-I6)*
- [ ] Retrieval returns grounded passages or an explicit insufficiency, never an empty list. *(REQ-R3)*
- [ ] The refusal threshold is read from `MIN_SCORE`, not written in code. *(REQ-R4)*
- [ ] Q1–Q10 in `EVALUATION.md` return the expected section in the top three. Any shortfall is recorded at stage 04, not tuned away. *(REQ-R5)*
- [ ] Q11–Q13 return insufficiency. *(REQ-R6)*
- [ ] A refusal does not call the model: `model.was_called` is false. *(REQ-R3, REQ-G2)*
- [ ] An answer is composed only from retrieved passages and carries those passages. *(REQ-G1, REQ-G3)*
- [ ] The prompt is a versioned file, and every answer records `prompt_version`, `model` and `latency_ms`. *(REQ-G4, REQ-G5)*
- [ ] With `GENERATION_ENABLED=false`, or with the provider failing, `/ask` returns `outcome: degraded`, the passages, and `answer: null`. *(REQ-G6, REQ-U6)*
- [ ] `POST /ask` returns exactly one of `answered`, `refused` or `degraded`, and validates against PRD §5. *(REQ-A1, REQ-A2)*
- [ ] Every turn is stored against its conversation. *(REQ-C1, REQ-C2)*
- [ ] Citations show document, section, version and status without a click. *(REQ-U2, REQ-U3, REQ-U4)*
- [ ] A refusal, an answer and an error each look different on screen. *(REQ-U5)*
- [ ] The empty state is centred, has a send control, and offers three example questions; clicking one asks it. *(REQ-U1, REQ-U1b)*
- [ ] The top bar shows only routes that exist. At F5 that is Reset alone. *(REQ-U9)*
- [ ] The interface is light theme only and works at phone width. *(REQ-U10, REQ-U11)*
- [ ] `/healthz` reports database, vector store and model, and still answers when Postgres or Qdrant is down. *(REQ-A5, NFR-5)*
- [ ] No secret appears in a log line, response or commit. *(NFR-6)*
