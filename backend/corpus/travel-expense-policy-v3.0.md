# Travel and Expense Policy

**Aldermere Bank plc — Group Finance**
Version 3.0 · Effective 1 April 2025 · Superseded 1 April 2026

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

### 1.1 Purpose

This policy sets out how Aldermere Bank employees incur, approve and reclaim expenses
arising from business travel and other business activity.

### 1.2 Who this applies to

All permanent employees, fixed-term employees, and contractors engaged directly by
Aldermere Bank, in every jurisdiction in which the Bank operates.

---

## 2. Principles

Expenditure must be necessary, proportionate to the business benefit, and supported by an
itemised receipt. The most economical option that adequately meets the business need is
the correct option.

---

## 3. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Business travel** | Travel undertaken for a business purpose away from the employee's normal place of work |
| **Normal place of work** | The location recorded as the employee's base in the HR system |
| **Line manager** | The individual recorded as the employee's direct reporting manager |

---

## 4. Approval

### 4.1 Approval thresholds

The following thresholds apply to a single claim:

| Claim value | Approval required |
|---|---|
| Below £50 | Automatic approval on submission |
| **£50 or more** | **Line-manager approval before reimbursement** |
| £500 or more | Line-manager approval and Department Head approval |
| £2,500 or more | Line-manager approval, Department Head approval and Group Finance approval |

### 4.2 Categories requiring approval regardless of value

- Client entertainment of any kind
- Alcohol
- Any expense incurred on a weekend or public holiday

### 4.3 Self-approval

No employee may approve their own claim.

---

## 5. Travel

### 5.1 Booking

All travel must be booked through the Bank's designated travel provider unless doing so
is demonstrably more expensive.

### 5.2 Air travel

| Flight duration | Permitted class |
|---|---|
| Under 4 hours | Economy |
| 4 to 8 hours | Economy |
| Over 8 hours | Premium economy. Business class with Department Head approval |

First class air travel is not permitted.

### 5.3 Rail travel

Standard class for all journeys. First class requires line-manager approval.

### 5.4 Ground transport

Public transport where practical. Taxis where public transport is not available or where
travel is between 23:00 and 05:00.

### 5.5 Personal vehicle

Mileage is reimbursed at 45 pence per mile for the first 10,000 miles in a financial year
and 25 pence per mile thereafter.

---

## 6. Accommodation and subsistence

### 6.1 Accommodation caps

| Location | Cap per night |
|---|---|
| London | £220 |
| Other UK cities | £160 |
| Singapore, Hong Kong, New York, Tokyo | £275 |
| All other locations | £180 |

Exceeding a cap requires Department Head approval.

### 6.2 Subsistence

| | Limit |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | £15 |
| Lunch | £20 |
| Evening meal | £35 |

### 6.3 Alcohol

Alcohol is reimbursable as part of a meal, up to £30 per person.

---

## 7. Making a claim

### 7.1 Deadline

Claims must be submitted within 90 days of the date the expense was incurred. Claims
submitted after 90 days require Department Head approval.

### 7.2 Lost receipts

Where an itemised receipt has been lost, the employee may submit a declaration. A
declaration may be used no more than three times in any rolling twelve-month period.

---

## 8. Breaches

A claim that is knowingly false or inflated is a disciplinary matter and may constitute
fraud.
