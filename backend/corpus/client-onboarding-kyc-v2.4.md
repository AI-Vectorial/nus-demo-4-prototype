# Client Onboarding and KYC Refresh Policy

**Aldermere Bank plc — Financial Crime Compliance**
Version 2.4 · Effective 15 January 2026 · Supersedes version 2.3

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

### 1.1 Purpose

This policy sets out the customer due diligence Aldermere Bank performs before entering a
business relationship, and the periodic review it performs thereafter. It gives effect to
the Bank's obligations under applicable anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorist
financing regulation.

### 1.2 Scope

This policy applies to all client relationships across Corporate Banking, Institutional
Banking and Private Banking, in every jurisdiction in which the Bank operates.

### 1.3 What this policy does not cover

- Transaction monitoring and suspicious activity reporting, covered by the Financial
  Crime Surveillance Policy
- Sanctions screening methodology, covered by the Sanctions Policy
- Retention of due diligence records, covered by the Records Retention and Data Handling
  Policy

---

## 2. Principles

### 2.1 No relationship without due diligence

No account may be opened, no facility drawn, and no transaction executed before customer
due diligence is complete and recorded. There is no provision for retrospective due
diligence.

### 2.2 Risk-based approach

The depth of due diligence is proportionate to the assessed risk of the relationship.
Higher risk requires more evidence, more senior approval and more frequent review.

### 2.3 Evidence over assertion

A client's statement about itself is not evidence. Every material fact recorded in a due
diligence file must be supported by a document from an independent source.

### 2.4 The relationship owner is accountable

The named relationship manager is accountable for the currency and completeness of the
client file, whether or not they personally assembled it.

## 3. Client risk rating

### 3.1 Rating at onboarding

Every client is assigned a risk rating at onboarding. The rating determines the due
diligence tier under section 4 and the refresh cycle under section 6.

| Rating | Typical characteristics |
|---|---|
| **Low** | UK or EEA incorporated, regulated entity, transparent ownership, domestic activity |
| **Standard** | Incorporated in a jurisdiction not on the Bank's elevated-risk list, ownership traceable within two layers |
| **High** | Ownership opaque or exceeding three layers; activity in an elevated-risk jurisdiction; cash-intensive business; politically exposed person connected |

### 3.2 Factors requiring a High rating

A client must be rated High where any of the following applies:

- A beneficial owner is a politically exposed person, or an immediate family member or
  close associate of one
- The client or a beneficial owner is resident or incorporated in a jurisdiction on the
  Bank's elevated-risk list
- The ownership structure includes a bearer-share entity or a trust whose beneficiaries
  cannot be identified
- The client has been the subject of an adverse media finding assessed as credible
- The client was previously exited by the Bank for financial crime reasons

### 3.3 Changing a rating

A rating may be lowered only with the approval of the Financial Crime Compliance
regional head, recorded with reasons in the client file. A rating may be raised by the
relationship manager without further approval and must be raised where section 3.2
applies.

## 4. Due diligence tiers

### 4.1 Standard due diligence

Required for all clients rated Low or Standard.

| Evidence required | |
|---|---|
| Legal identity | Certificate of incorporation or equivalent constitutional document |
| Registered address | Independent confirmation, dated within 3 months |
| Ownership | Identification of all beneficial owners holding 25% or more |
| Control | Identification of directors and any individual exercising significant control |
| Purpose | A recorded statement of the intended nature and purpose of the relationship |
| Source of funds | A recorded explanation, proportionate to the expected activity |
| Screening | Sanctions, PEP and adverse media screening on the entity and all identified individuals |

### 4.2 Enhanced due diligence

Required for all clients rated High. Includes everything in section 4.1, plus:

| Additional evidence | |
|---|---|
| Ownership | Identification of all beneficial owners holding **10% or more** |
| Source of wealth | Documented evidence of how the beneficial owners' wealth was accumulated, not merely its current source |
| Structure | A full ownership chart to ultimate beneficial ownership, whatever the number of layers |
| Rationale | A written explanation of why the structure takes the form it does |
| Approval | Written approval by a Financial Crime Compliance regional head |
| Site visit | For cash-intensive businesses, a recorded visit to the principal place of business |

### 4.3 Simplified due diligence

Simplified due diligence may be applied only to a client that is itself a credit or
financial institution regulated in the United Kingdom or the EEA, and only with the
approval of Financial Crime Compliance. It comprises the evidence in section 4.1 with the
exception of beneficial ownership identification, where reliance may be placed on the
client's own regulatory status.

Simplified due diligence may not be applied to any client rated High, regardless of
regulatory status.

## 5. Politically exposed persons

### 5.1 Identification

A politically exposed person is an individual entrusted with a prominent public function,
their immediate family members, and their known close associates. The Bank's screening
provider maintains the reference list.

### 5.2 Requirements

Where a PEP is identified as a beneficial owner or controller:

- The client must be rated High and subject to enhanced due diligence
- Source of wealth must be documented for the PEP specifically
- Approval must be obtained from a Financial Crime Compliance regional head before the
  relationship is established
- The relationship is subject to annual review regardless of any other factor

### 5.3 Ceasing to be a PEP

An individual who ceases to hold a prominent public function remains treated as a PEP for
a minimum of twelve months thereafter. Continuing to treat the individual as a PEP beyond
twelve months is a matter for Financial Crime Compliance judgement, having regard to the
seniority of the former function and the individual's continuing influence.

## 6. Periodic refresh

### 6.1 Refresh cycles

| Client rating | Refresh interval |
|---|---|
| Low | Every 5 years |
| Standard | Every 3 years |
| High | **Every 12 months** |

The interval runs from the date the previous review was completed and signed off, not
from the date it was due.

### 6.2 What a refresh comprises

A refresh re-verifies the evidence required by the client's current due diligence tier. It
is not a confirmation that nothing has changed; it is a re-collection of evidence.

Where a document obtained at onboarding remains valid and current — for example a
certificate of incorporation — it need not be re-obtained, but its continuing validity must
be confirmed and recorded.

### 6.3 Event-driven review

A refresh is triggered ahead of its scheduled date where any of the following occurs:

- A change in beneficial ownership of 10% or more
- A change in the client's registered jurisdiction
- A credible adverse media finding
- A sanctions screening alert that is not resolved as a false positive
- A material change in the nature or volume of activity
- The client becomes connected to a politically exposed person

### 6.4 Overdue refresh

Where a refresh becomes overdue:

| Days overdue | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 to 30 | Relationship manager notified. No restriction |
| 31 to 60 | Department Head notified. No new facilities may be extended |
| 61 to 90 | Financial Crime Compliance notified. Outbound payments restricted |
| Over 90 | **The relationship is suspended pending completion** |

A suspension under this section may be lifted only by Financial Crime Compliance.

---

## 7. Records

### 7.1 The client file

Every relationship has a single client file holding all due diligence evidence, screening
results, risk rating decisions, approvals and review records.

### 7.2 Retention

Due diligence records are retained in accordance with the Records Retention and Data
Handling Policy. Relationship managers should note that retention obligations continue
after a relationship ends.

---

## 8. Exceptions

### 8.1 No exception to section 2.1

There is no exception to the requirement that due diligence is complete before a
relationship is established. A request to open an account ahead of completion is refused.

### 8.2 Other exceptions

Any other exception requires the written approval of the Group Head of Financial Crime
Compliance, recorded with reasons, and is reported to the Financial Crime Committee at its
next meeting.
