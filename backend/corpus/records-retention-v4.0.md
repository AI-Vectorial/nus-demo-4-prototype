# Records Retention and Data Handling Policy

**Aldermere Bank plc — Group Legal and Company Secretariat**
Version 4.0 · Effective 1 February 2026 · Supersedes version 3.2

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

### 1.1 Purpose

This policy sets out how long Aldermere Bank retains records, how those records must be
held, and how they are disposed of. It gives effect to the Bank's statutory,
regulatory and contractual retention obligations, and to its obligation not to retain
personal data for longer than is necessary.

### 1.2 Scope

This policy applies to all records created or received by the Bank in the course of its
business, in any format, held on any system, in any jurisdiction — including records held
on the Bank's behalf by a third party.

### 1.3 What a record is

A record is any information created, received or maintained as evidence of a business
activity or transaction. Format is irrelevant: a message in a chat application is a
record if it evidences a business activity, and a document saved to a personal drive is a
record of the Bank, not of the employee.

---

## 2. Principles

### 2.1 Retain what is required, for as long as required

Records are retained for the period specified in section 4 and are then destroyed.
Retention beyond the specified period is a breach of this policy as much as destruction
before it.

### 2.2 Destruction is the default

At the end of a retention period, a record is destroyed unless a legal hold under section
6 applies. There is no provision for indefinite retention "in case it is needed".

### 2.3 One authoritative copy

Each record has one authoritative copy in a designated system. Working copies are not
records and must not be retained once the authoritative copy exists.

### 2.4 Accountability sits with the business

The business function that creates a record owns it. Group Legal maintains this policy;
it does not maintain the records.

## 3. Classification

### 3.1 Classification levels

| Level | Meaning | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| **Public** | May be disclosed without restriction | No restriction |
| **Internal** | For Bank staff generally | Not to be sent outside the Bank without approval |
| **Confidential** | Restricted to those with a business need | Encryption in transit and at rest. Access logged |
| **Highly Confidential** | Material non-public information, client identifying data, personal data of clients | Encryption in transit and at rest. Access logged and reviewed. Named access list |

### 3.2 Client data

All records identifying a client, or capable of identifying a client in combination with
other information reasonably available, are **Highly Confidential** by default.

### 3.3 Downgrading

A classification may be lowered only by the record owner, with a recorded reason. Client
identifying data may not be downgraded while the client relationship subsists.

## 4. Retention periods

### 4.1 General table

| Record category | Retention period | Period runs from |
|---|---|---|
| Board and committee minutes | Permanent | — |
| Statutory accounts and audit files | 10 years | End of financial year |
| **Client correspondence** | **7 years** | **End of the client relationship** |
| Client due diligence records | 7 years | End of the client relationship |
| Transaction records | 7 years | Date of transaction |
| Credit files | 7 years | Facility maturity or repayment |
| Complaints | 6 years | Date of final response |
| Expense claims and supporting receipts | 6 years | End of the tax year in which incurred |
| Employee personnel files | 6 years | End of employment |
| Recruitment records for unsuccessful candidates | 12 months | Date of decision |
| CCTV | 31 days | Date of recording |
| Access logs to Highly Confidential systems | 2 years | Date of access |
| Telephone recordings, trading floor | 7 years | Date of recording |
| Telephone recordings, all other | 6 months | Date of recording |

### 4.2 Client correspondence — what it includes

Client correspondence means any communication between the Bank and a client, or about a
client, including email, letters, meeting notes, recorded calls and messages in approved
messaging channels.

The seven-year period runs from the end of the client relationship, **not** from the date
of the individual communication. A letter sent in the first year of a fifteen-year
relationship is retained for twenty-two years in total.

### 4.3 Where two periods apply

Where a record falls into more than one category, **the longer period applies**.

### 4.4 Records held by third parties

Records created or held on the Bank's behalf by a third-party service provider remain the
Bank's records and are subject to the retention periods in section 4.1. The contractual
arrangements governing such records are set out in the Outsourcing and Third-Party Risk
Policy.

## 5. Storage and access

### 5.1 Approved systems

Records must be held in a system on the Bank's approved systems register. A record held
only on a local drive, a personal device, or an unapproved cloud service is a breach of
this policy regardless of its classification.

### 5.2 Access

Access to Confidential and Highly Confidential records is granted on the basis of
business need and is reviewed at least annually. Access granted for a specific purpose is
withdrawn when that purpose ends.

### 5.3 Transfer outside the Bank

Confidential and Highly Confidential records may be transferred outside the Bank only
where there is a lawful basis, an approved transfer mechanism, and the recipient is
subject to equivalent obligations.

### 5.4 Cross-border transfer

Transfer of personal data across a border requires an approved transfer mechanism
recorded before the transfer occurs. Group Legal maintains the register of approved
mechanisms by jurisdiction.

---

## 6. Legal hold

### 6.1 Effect

Where a legal hold is imposed, destruction of the records in scope stops immediately,
irrespective of any retention period that would otherwise apply. A legal hold overrides
every other provision of this policy.

### 6.2 Imposing a hold

Only Group Legal may impose or lift a legal hold. A hold is recorded in the legal hold
register with its scope and the reason.

### 6.3 Obligations while a hold is in force

An employee notified of a legal hold must not delete, alter or move any record in scope,
including drafts and working copies that would not otherwise be records.

---

## 7. Destruction

### 7.1 Method

Destruction must be irreversible and appropriate to the classification. Confidential and
Highly Confidential records require certified destruction with a certificate retained for
two years.

### 7.2 Record of destruction

Destruction of any record category is logged: what was destroyed, when, by whom, and
under what authority. The destruction log is itself retained for six years.

---

## 8. Breaches

Retaining a record beyond its period, destroying one before its period ends, or holding
one in an unapproved system are each breaches of this policy and are reportable to Group
Legal.

Destruction of a record subject to a legal hold is a serious matter and is reported to
the Audit Committee.
