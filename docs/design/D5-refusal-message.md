# D5 — What a refusal says

**Decided:** 2026-10-08, table 3, stage 02 decision interview
**Status:** locked

## What we chose

Option B — a fixed refusal plus the corpus directory.

The refusal says the available policies do not support the question, then lists the
documented policy areas and their owners, taken from corpus metadata. It makes no model
call.

## Why

A — a single generic message — is too vague to say where to go. C — naming the owner of
the closest passage — effectively says "the closest thing I found must be the right
owner", right after deciding the evidence is insufficient.

## Consequences

Satisfies C6 and U5, and shapes how the product feels when it says no.

## Careful about

Never invent an owner, team, email address or contact route. If nothing in the corpus
identifies an appropriate owner, say so rather than guess.
